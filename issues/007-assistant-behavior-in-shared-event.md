## Question

How does the Assistant behave when several Members share one Event's Chat and Canvas?

- When does it speak: only when mentioned, when a Member asks about a selected item, or also on its own (for example when a Member adds an Inspiration)?
- Do its changes apply directly, or arrive as suggestions a Member accepts? Does that differ between requested and unrequested changes, or for changes to the budget?
- How are its changes attributed, shown and undone (the edit history)?
- How does it treat content Members bring in (shared posts, pasted links, fetched pages) that may carry instructions?
