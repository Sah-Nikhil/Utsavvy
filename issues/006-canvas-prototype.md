## Question

How should the Canvas look and behave? Settle it by reacting to a rough, clickable prototype that covers:

- The desktop layout: the Canvas with the Chat beside it.
- The mobile layout: the Chat as the main view, plus a compact Canvas tab.
- The kinds of item on the Canvas (budget, timeline, checklist, Vendor, Inspiration, note) and how they are arranged: structured sections or a freeform infinite canvas.
- Selecting an item to ask the Assistant about it, and the Assistant pointing back by highlighting the items it means.
- "Add to Canvas" from a Chat message, an Inspiration result and a Vendor card.
- Comments on an item, and presence dots.
