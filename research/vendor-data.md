# Vendor data sources for ticket 002

Checked 2026-10-21. Scope: an India-first Event planning Assistant with Vendor Markers and three distinct price labels: **Listed price** (public source and date seen), **Estimate** (Assistant generated), and **Quote** (entered by a Member). This is a product and terms review, not a legal opinion. No paid API calls, account registrations, or listing extraction were made.

## Decision

**Inference.** For a five-build-day v1, use **Jaipur**, then **Delhi NCR** if time permits. Put a small, hand-verified set of Venue, caterer, decorator, and photographer Markers on an OSM-based map from OSM records or independently verified vendor-provided coordinates. Show **Listed price** only from a vendor's own public price page or a vendor-supplied price sheet whose use is permitted; record the exact URL, unit, scope, and `seen_at` date. Otherwise show an explicitly labeled **Estimate** or no price. Keep Member-entered **Quotes** separate. Do not import prices or coordinates from Google Maps or wedding directories into this database. The evidence below explains the limits of those alternatives. [OSM license](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), [Google Maps service terms](https://cloud.google.com/maps-platform/terms/maps-service-terms), [WedMeGood terms](https://www.wedmegood.com/terms), [Gemini Search terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms).

## Google Places API (New)

**Observation.** Google defines `banquet_hall`, `event_venue`, `wedding_venue`, `community_center`, and `catering_service` place types. It does not list a dedicated `photographer`, `wedding_decorator`, or `marriage_garden` type; text queries are needed for those phrases, with location bias and manual relevance review. A catering business may be a pure service-area business without a public storefront Marker. [Place types](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/place-types), [Text Search](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/text-search), [place fields](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/data-fields).

**Spot-check, no API request.** Public pages show the category vocabulary is relevant in [Delhi NCR](https://www.wedmegood.com/vendors/delhi-ncr/wedding-venues/all/marriage-garden-lawns/), [Mumbai](https://www.wedmegood.com/vendors/mumbai/wedding-venues/), [Bengaluru](https://www.wedmegood.com/vendors/bangalore/wedding-venues/), and [Jaipur](https://www.wedmegood.com/vendors/jaipur/wedding-venues/all/marriage-garden-lawns/). A [public Google Maps result in Jaipur](https://www.google.com/maps/search/4%2C%20Hare%20Krishna%20Regency%2C%20Kesar%20Nagar%2C%20Muhana%20Mandi%20Road%2C%20%20Jaipur%20India%20302020) includes wedding venues. **Inference.** Places is likely useful for discovery in these cities, but neither indexed pages nor Google's type list prove API result counts, category completeness, or price availability. No quantitative Places coverage claim is made.

**Observation.** A Text Search field mask such as `places.id,places.displayName,places.formattedAddress,places.location,places.primaryType,places.googleMapsUri` reaches **Text Search Pro** because name, coordinates, address, and type are Pro on search. `priceLevel` or `priceRange` reaches **Enterprise**. `priceLevel` is a coarse enum; `priceRange` is a money range without a wedding package, guest count, veg/non-veg, per-plate, or function-rental unit. Neither field is a defensible Listed price for a Venue or another event Vendor. They can be absent. [Field/SKU table](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/data-fields), [price fields schema](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/reference/rest/v1/places), [field mask billing](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/usage-and-billing).

**Observation, 2026-10-21 price list.** Prices below are USD per **1,000 billable events after the monthly free cap**, first paid tier. India's price list applies only to accounts eligible for India billing and primary use; do not assume eligibility solely from the developer's location. [India eligibility FAQ](https://developers.google.com/maps/billing-and-pricing/india), [India list](https://developers.google.com/maps/billing-and-pricing/pricing-india), [global list](https://developers.google.com/maps/billing-and-pricing/pricing).

**Observation.** A `place_id` can be stored indefinitely; other Places content cannot generally be prefetched or persisted, while Places latitude/longitude may be cached for at most 30 days. Google requires attribution and says Places results displayed **on a map** must use a Google Map. Its service terms also prohibit using Places content with a non-Google map. **Inference.** Putting Google-derived Vendor Markers on Leaflet with OSM or CARTO tiles is not an acceptable v1 path. Places content can be shown without any map with required attribution, or on a Google Map, but that is a separate integration and does not authorize building a persistent independent Vendor directory. [Places policies](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/policies), [service-specific terms, Places §14](https://cloud.google.com/maps-platform/terms/maps-service-terms), [general Maps terms](https://cloud.google.com/maps-platform/terms).

**Observation.** The separate **Places UI Kit** has an express exception permitting use alongside a non-Google map, with attribution and export/caching limits. It is not permission to take Places API results and draw independent Leaflet Markers. [Service-specific terms, Places UI Kit §15](https://cloud.google.com/maps-platform/terms/maps-service-terms), [UI Kit pricing](https://developers.google.com/maps/billing-and-pricing/pricing-india).

## Gemini grounding

**Observation, Gemini API Grounding with Google Maps.** It produces location-aware prose with Google Maps place citations/links. The current Interactions API returns `place_citation` name and URL annotations and optionally accepts user latitude/longitude. It is not a structured bulk Vendor identity, coordinates, or wedding price feed. The associated Maps source must immediately follow the supported answer, be visible within one interaction, and link to Google Maps with attribution. Google restricts storage of Maps-grounded results to 90 days for display evaluation or six months in that end user's chat history, and bars scraping/exporting Maps data. [Current Maps grounding docs](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/maps-grounding), [Gemini additional terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms).

**Observation, widget distinction.** The current Gemini Interactions API documents inline Maps citations and no required context widget token. Older Firebase/Vertex `generateContent` metadata can contain `googleMapsWidgetContextToken`, for a `PlacesContextElement`/context widget; Vertex has a separate `enable_widget` option. Do not copy Vertex widget instructions into the current Gemini API flow. [Gemini Maps docs](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/maps-grounding), [Firebase metadata reference](https://firebase.google.com/docs/reference/js/ai.groundingmetadata), [Vertex API reference](https://docs.cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/docs/reference/rpc/google.cloud.aiplatform.v1).

**Observation, Google Search grounding.** Gemini can execute multiple web queries and returns source-linked answer annotations plus a `search_suggestions` UI snippet. Its terms require the grounded result and associated Search Suggestions to be shown to the end user who prompted it; they forbid collecting grounded links/results to build an index or scraping target pages. Limited chat-history storage is allowed under stated conditions. Thus a cited price sentence in an on-demand Chat answer is possible, but using grounding to populate persistent Canvas Listed prices or a price database is not supported by these terms. The Assistant must still verify that the cited page really states the amount and unit; a model answer alone is not a public price source. [Search grounding docs](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/google-search), [Gemini terms, Search](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms).

**Observation, availability and cost.** Gemini API lists India as a supported region; Maps grounding is globally scoped but its documentation says availability may vary and currently supports English prompts/responses. Google Search grounding works in available languages. For Gemini 3 and newer paid-tier models, both grounding tools list **5,000 free tool search requests/month**, then **$14/1,000 search queries** (one prompt may run several queries), plus model tokens; model-specific free-tier grounding availability varies. For Gemini 2.5 Flash, the pricing table instead lists Maps **500 requests/day on free tier**, or **1,500/day included on paid tier then $25/1,000 grounded prompts**; Search **500/day free tier**, or **1,500/day included on paid tier then $35/1,000 grounded prompts**. Check the chosen model and tier before implementation. [Regions](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/available-regions), [Maps grounding](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/maps-grounding), [Search grounding](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/google-search), [Gemini pricing](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing).

## OpenStreetMap, Overpass, and basemaps

**Observation.** `amenity=events_venue` is the documented wedding/banquet facility tag; `amenity=community_centre` may capture Indian kalyan mandaps and community halls; `events_venue=yes` can identify a secondary use. `amenity=banquet_hall` is **not** the documented primary tag. Query named nodes/ways/relations and use `out center` for way/relation Marker coordinates, then deduplicate and review them. OSM does not offer a wedding price field in these tag definitions. [Events venue tag](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Tag:amenity%3Devents_venue), [community centre tag](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Tag:amenity%3Dcommunity_centre), [secondary events venue tag](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Key:events_venue).

**Coverage test.** Small public Overpass count requests for Delhi and Jaipur were attempted on 2026-10-21: `overpass.kumi.systems` timed out at 28 seconds, and `overpass-api.de` returned HTTP 406 on POST and HTTP 502 on GET. No reliable India/city count was obtained; do not present a guessed number. A later manual test can run `[out:json][timeout:15];nwr["amenity"="events_venue"](26.82,75.70,26.98,75.90);out count;` for a **small Jaipur bbox** and repeat with `community_centre`, using the public instance's fair-use limits. [Overpass public-instance guidance](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Overpass_API), [Overpass rate/timeout description](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Overpass_API/Wording). **Inference.** Expect incomplete venue coverage and weaker caterer/decorator/photographer coverage; verify each chosen Marker against a first-party page or Member knowledge before a demo.

**Observation.** OSM data is commercially reusable under ODbL with attribution and share-alike obligations for a derived database. The standard `tile.openstreetmap.org` service permits normal interactive viewing, requires identification/attribution/caching, and prohibits prefetch, bulk download, and offline use; it is a community service, not a production tile SLA. Public Nominatim has a 1 request/second ceiling, identification and attribution requirements, and disallows use as a generic no-code/vibe-coded geocoder; avoid it for production vendor geocoding. [OSM copyright/license](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), [tile policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/), [Nominatim policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/nominatim/).

**Observation, CARTO change in September 2026.** CARTO now requires a CARTO-issued basemap key and visible `© OpenStreetMap contributors, © CARTO` attribution. Free basemaps allow up to **5 million tiles/month for non-commercial use** and **1 million tiles/month for commercial use**; CARTO may suspend free access. Its commercial plan begins at **$500/month** for 10 million tiles/month. **Inference.** A public hackathon demo might be non-commercial, but do not assume that an eventual product is. Requesting a key is a signup step not performed for this research; choose a provider whose current terms fit deployment. [CARTO terms](https://carto.com/legal/basemap-terms/), [CARTO attribution](https://carto.com/attribution/), [key request page](https://dashboard.basemaps.carto.com/).

## First-party Listed prices and v1 implementation

**Observation.** A Jaipur venue's own [Parampara Banquet Hall page](https://virasatjaipur.com/venues/parampara-banquet-hall) publicly states **approximately ₹1,200-₹3,500 per plate** for 200-400 guests, and the same operator's [venues page](https://virasatjaipur.com/venues) states a **₹1,000-₹3,000 per-plate** range for Parampara Garden. A caterer's own [Vinayak Foods & Catering page](https://www.vinayakfoodsncatering.in/) states **starting at ₹250 per plate**, while its FAQ says the exact amount depends on menu and scale. These are examples of citable first-party price pages, observed 2026-10-21, not confirmed offers or Quotes. They show why the approximate/starting qualifier, amount, unit, guest-count condition, and source date must travel together.

**Inference and build slice.** Curate roughly 12-20 Jaipur Vendors across four categories, adding Delhi NCR only after the Jaipur map works. For each Marker, store `vendor_id`, category, display name, independently sourced latitude/longitude, coordinate source, and verification date. Use OSM coordinates where present, or manually position a Marker on an OSM map after checking the Vendor's own address; mark a caterer/decorator/photographer whose only known location is a city as a service area in the list instead of inventing a storefront Marker. For a Listed price, store the first-party URL, amount/range, INR, unit (`per_plate`, `per_day`, or `per_function`), approximate/starting qualifier, relevant veg/non-veg and minimum-guest/package conditions, and `seen_at`; show the source link and date beside it. Treat unavailable or ambiguous prices as no Listed price. Use Assistant-generated category budgets only as **Estimate**, labeled with assumptions, and Member-entered prices only as **Quote**. Link discovery results out to vendor or directory pages, without booking/payment workflows. [First-party venue example](https://virasatjaipur.com/venues/parampara-banquet-hall), [first-party caterer example](https://www.vinayakfoodsncatering.in/), [OSM license](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), [Places restrictions](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/policies).

**Risks.** OSM's live venue coverage is unmeasured here because Overpass failed, and service Vendors may have only service areas rather than suitable physical Markers. First-party prices can become stale or omit taxes, decor, rentals, date, and guest minimums; a nightly refresh is not justified for the demo, so show `seen_at` and invite Members to enter actual Quotes. A first-party public page can still restrict copying in its own terms; keep the curated record to facts, cite and link the page, and seek permission if expanding this into systematic ingestion. Gemini grounding is useful for live conversational discovery with its required citations, but its restrictions make it a poor source for a persistent Vendor/Listed-price database. [OSM events venue tag](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Tag:amenity%3Devents_venue), [Gemini terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms), [Google Places policies](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/policies).
