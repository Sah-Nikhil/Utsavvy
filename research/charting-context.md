# Event-planning assistant research

Checked 2026-10-21. **Observation** means a source states or shows the fact. **Inference** means a design conclusion from cited observations. Rules from one PromptWars event must not be assumed to govern another.

## 1. PromptWars, 2026

The Virtual page currently says the program is undergoing a refresh and gives no next launch date. The in-person page says its program is being restructured and new cities will be announced. ## 2. Google Labs Play with Putty

- **Observation:** Play with Putty is a waitlisted Google Labs research experiment in real-time collaborative “vibe coding.” Users share one live building environment, select an interface element and instruct it in natural language, and inspect shared version history/ledger. Google Research says models generate whole interfaces from natural-language prompts and multiple people edit together. Neither cited Google page names a specific underlying model or Gemini version for Putty. [Labs page](https://labs.google/playwithputty), [Google Research, 14 Oct 2026](https://research.google/blog/research-2026-10-ways-were-amplifying-human-ingenuity/).
- **Inference:** For an event planner, use its *interaction pattern*: select a budget row, timeline item or vendor card and ask for a precise change; show the change live and keep an understandable history. Collaborative editing could serve family/co-organizer decisions. This is UX inspiration, not evidence that Putty supplies a reusable API. [Labs page](https://labs.google/playwithputty), [Google Research](https://research.google/blog/research-2026-10-ways-were-amplifying-human-ingenuity/).

## 3. Google Labs CC

- **Observation:** `https://labs.google.com/cc/landing` resolves to a Google sign-in screen in the public web fetch, so the product analysis uses Google's official announcements instead. CC began as a Gemini-built agent that reads opted-in Gmail, Calendar, Drive and web context, emails a daily “Your Day Ahead” brief, prepares email drafts/calendar links, and accepts direction by reply/email. [Landing](https://labs.google.com/cc/landing), [Google announcement, Dec 2025](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-labs/cc-ai-agent/).
- **Observation:** Google expanded CC in September 2026 to families/groups of up to six. Members choose what to share; the agent has its own account and permissions, consolidates shared dates/tasks into a daily brief, requests missing details, remembers group versus personal preferences, and asks permission before external action. Google says it uses an isolated cloud computer, Antigravity agentic harness, latest Gemini models, and can use Maps API drive times or create shared Docs/Sheets. The family version is an early experiment for US adults with personal Google accounts. [Google family CC announcement, Sep 2026](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-labs/cc-expanding-to-groups/).
- **Inference:** Reuse the pattern of a proactive event briefing: “next 7 days,” budget decisions, unbooked vendors, overdue tasks, and which co-organizer owns each item. Let people forward a quote/invitation into the plan, keep shared and private context distinct, and require confirmation before contacting a vendor or booking. CC's US availability means it is reference UX, not an India-ready dependency. [Google announcement](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-labs/cc-ai-agent/), [family CC announcement](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-labs/cc-expanding-to-groups/).

## 4. SahayakOS map reuse

- **Observation:** SahayakOS coordinates NGOs and volunteers for crisis/community service. It uses Next.js 16/React 19/TypeScript, Tailwind 4 and shadcn, Convex, Clerk, and **Leaflet**. `package.json` also lists `@google/genai`, but the map implementation is Leaflet, not Google Maps/Mapbox/MapLibre. [README](https://github.com/Sah-Nikhil/SahayakOS/blob/master/README.md), [package.json](https://github.com/Sah-Nikhil/SahayakOS/blob/master/package.json), [map source](https://github.com/Sah-Nikhil/SahayakOS/blob/master/components/city-map.tsx).
- **Observation:** The home `CityMap` uses CARTO light tiles based on OpenStreetMap data, two tile layers for basemap and labels, Leaflet circle markers, count tooltips, marker-click `flyTo`, custom zoom controls and optional city bounds. The opportunity map uses standard OSM tiles, city/skill/urgency filters, fit-to-marker bounds, marker selection, and a side detail panel with opportunity cards. Marker coordinates come from `ngo.hqLocation.lat/lng` stored in Convex and returned by `getMapData`, not a live vendor/place search. [CityMap](https://github.com/Sah-Nikhil/SahayakOS/blob/master/components/city-map.tsx), [opportunity map](https://github.com/Sah-Nikhil/SahayakOS/blob/master/components/ngo-opportunity-map.tsx), [Convex query](https://github.com/Sah-Nikhil/SahayakOS/blob/master/convex/queries.ts).
- **Inference:** Reuse the map/list selection, filters, custom marker styling, bounds, and detail-card structure for venues/vendors. Replace NGO/opportunity types and source records with venue/vendor records; add a separate verified source for vendor identities, locations, prices and availability. A Leaflet basemap alone does not provide current vendor listings or validate quotes. [Opportunity map](https://github.com/Sah-Nikhil/SahayakOS/blob/master/components/ngo-opportunity-map.tsx), [Convex query](https://github.com/Sah-Nikhil/SahayakOS/blob/master/convex/queries.ts).

## 5. Comparable products and gap

| Product | Observed feature to learn from | Inference for this product |
|---|---|---|
| [WedMeGood](https://www.wedmegood.com/) | India-focused vendor profiles with prices/reviews, inspiration gallery, vendor shortlist and checklist; its app also advertises budget and guest-list tools. [App overview](https://www.wedmegood.com/blog/the-ultimate-wedding-planning-app-that-you-must-try/). | Vendor trust and visual idea saving are table stakes for weddings; add explicit quote provenance and budget fit. |
| [WeddingWire India](https://www.weddingwire.in/my-wedding-planner) | Linked budget, editable checklist/deadlines, guest list/RSVP, wedding site and vendor manager; vendor search can filter category and geography. [Vendor manager](https://www.weddingwire.in/wedding-tips/vendor-manager-tool--c10453). | Keep budget, tasks and vendor decisions synchronized in one plan. |
| [WeddingBazaar (former ShaadiSaga)](https://www.weddingbazaar.com/about-us) | Vendor marketplace, planning ideas and assisted booking through a personal wedding manager; the official vendor app says ShaadiSaga became WeddingBazaar. [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.shaadisaga.vendorapp). | Human-assisted vendor selection remains a meaningful competitor; show why AI suggestions were made and leave room for manual choice. |
| [Zola](https://www.zola.com/wedding-planning/app) | US-oriented wedding website, registry, editable checklist, budget, guest list/RSVP and guest text messaging; checklist adapts to wedding date. [Checklist](https://www.zola.com/expert-advice/your-ultimate-wedding-planning-checklist). | Date-driven task generation and guest coordination are useful; localize to Indian event types and prices. |
| [Wedd.ai](https://www.wedd.ai/) | India-focused AI wedding product advertises extracting a vendor quote into a task and budget amount. | Quote-to-plan automation already exists; differentiation cannot rest on generic “AI wedding planner.” |
| [Ekatraa](https://ekatraa.in/) | India AI event marketplace advertises verified vendors, secure bookings, AI plans, recommendations and agreements. | A cross-event AI/vendor claim already exists; prove live usefulness across weddings, birthdays and community events. |

**Inferred gap:** Large incumbents concentrate on weddings and marketplaces, while newer AI products advertise planning automation. A useful narrower wedge is an editable, budget-constrained plan spanning *multiple* Indian event types, with source-labeled vendor quotes, task dependencies, co-organizer ownership and proactive next actions. This is a synthesis of the product descriptions, not a measured market absence. [WedMeGood](https://www.wedmegood.com/), [WeddingWire India](https://www.weddingwire.in/my-wedding-planner), [WeddingBazaar](https://www.weddingbazaar.com/about-us), [Wedd.ai](https://www.wedd.ai/), [Ekatraa](https://ekatraa.in/).
